# CodexGun — Mac public beta 0.1.1 beta 2

Native Rust arcade shooter for Apple Silicon Macs. Play offline after installation.

## Download and install

Download `CodexGun-0.1.1-beta.2-Apple-Silicon.dmg`, open it, drag CodexGun into Applications, eject the disk, then open CodexGun from Applications. The menu explains the controls.

- Apple Silicon (M1 or later); macOS 13 or later. Intel Macs are not supported.
- Version 0.1.1, bundle build 4, beta 2.
- Download size: 58,041,110 bytes (58.0 MB).
- Signed by Adevious Ltd and notarized by Apple.
- SHA-256: `623e344a6ce1994421f0371f22a279c317a37de6b9a8ff03b3dce078bbc0b985`.

## This beta

Pressure hazards run through all four waves of all five sectors, with colour/timing guidance and touch-to-rescue instructions. Later waves retain their normal enemies, bosses and completion rules. Timers reset between waves and pause outside active play. Developer overlays and encounter shortcuts are excluded from the public app.
